Vektor: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 October 2026. Developer: The Zeus Labs, Islamabad, Pakistan. Contact: contact.zeuslabs@gmail.com

What the game stores

Your progress (levels cleared, stars, hints, daily streak, sound and vibration settings) is stored only on your device. We do not run servers, we do not create accounts and we do not collect your name, email or location.

Advertising

Vektor shows ads through Google AdMob: a banner on the home and level screens, and optional rewarded videos you choose to watch for an extra heart or hints. AdMob may collect your device's advertising ID, IP address and app interaction data to show and measure ads. In the EEA, UK and Switzerland you are asked for consent through Google's consent form first, and you can change your choice at any time in Settings > Ad privacy choices. Google's policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

Purchases

The optional "Remove ads" purchase is handled entirely by Google Play. We receive only a confirmation that the purchase belongs to your account; we never see your payment details.

Sharing

When you tap "Share my map", the game creates an image of the level on your device and hands it to the app you pick. Nothing is uploaded by Vektor.

Children

Vektor is not directed at children under 13 and is listed for a general audience.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
